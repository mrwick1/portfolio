Building a voice-AI product

At Qilin Labs, I built a voice-AI product and owned implementation, testing, and reliability work. This note describes my contribution while keeping the product’s implementation details private.

IMy contribution

I worked from product requirements through implementation and delivery. My responsibilities covered building the product, testing its behavior, and investigating reliability problems.

This work forms part of my broader experience across AI, web, and mobile products. It is the voice product’s delivery and reliability work that I want to describe here.

IIReliability

Reliability work was part of my role alongside implementation. Building a feature also meant checking its behavior and investigating failures.

The questions I bring to this work are practical: what should the user experience, what happens when something fails, and what evidence shows that a change helped? These questions guide how I review a product.

IIIHow I approach delivery

I start by clarifying the problem and the expected behavior with the people involved. I use that understanding to define a small first delivery and the checks it needs.

I review the result, investigate failures, and refine the work. This is the approach I bring to an engineering team or a client engagement.

IVWhat this note covers

This is a summary of my role. The product architecture, service choices, customer information, internal results, and implementation details remain private.

For examples where I can share implementation details, see my Figma verification tool and motorcycle navigation app.
