VApproach

Understand the problem

I talk to the people who use the product and the team that runs it. We agree on the problem, the constraints, and what a useful first delivery should do.

Deliver a working first step

I build a small, usable part of the product on the team’s existing platform. This lets us check the workflow and integrations early, before adding more features.

Test, refine, and hand over

I test the important paths and review the result with the team. I leave the code, documentation, and a walkthrough so they can maintain and extend the work.
